Privacy Policy — Colour Bricks Crush

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Short version: Colour Bricks Crush koi personal data collect nahi karta, koi data internet par nahi bhejta, aur koi ads ya tracking nahi hai.

1. What information do we collect?

None. The game does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data. There are no user accounts on any server, no analytics, and no advertising identifiers.

2. What is stored on your device?

Game progress (levels unlocked, stars, coins, boosters, player names you type and missions) is saved only on your own device using your browser/app local storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

3. Internet access

The game works fully offline. Any internet use is only for loading the game itself and its updates.

4. Third parties

No third-party advertising, analytics, or social networks are used inside the game. No data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected.

5. Children's privacy

The game does not collect any data from anyone, including children. There is no chat, no user-generated content sharing, and no external links inside the game.

6. Permissions

The app requests only the permissions needed to run as an installed web app (Progressive Web App). It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or files.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this same page.
